A Canvas style course management platform, built by a team and deployed to AWS.

Students register for course sections and view their grades. Instructors manage sections, enter assignments, and post grades. All of it runs against a relational database behind a REST API.

We split it into three services so the teams could build and ship independently: a **[registrar API](https://github.com/cst438-team03-org0/cst438-team-03-registrar)** and a **[gradebook API](https://github.com/cst438-team03-org0/cst438-team03-gradebook)** in Java, and a **[JavaScript frontend](https://github.com/cst438-team03-org0/cst438-team03-frontend)** on top of both. Deployed to AWS EC2 rather than left running on a laptop, which is where most of the real configuration work lived.
