Worked through the whole data science pipeline in Python, one lab at a time. NumPy arrays and vectorized operations first, then pandas for loading, grouping, and aggregating real datasets like bike sharing and campaign finance.

After that came plotting distributions, cleaning messy data, and training models. Linear regression, logistic regression, and a diabetes classifier checked with cross validation.

The modeling code is short. Getting the data into shape to run it is most of the job.
