*Taken in place of CST 315 Introduction to Cybersecurity under the approved elective substitution.*

Python labs and notebooks that walk the full data science pipeline in order. NumPy arrays and vectorized operations first, then pandas for loading, grouping, and aggregating real datasets like bike sharing and campaign finance.

From there it moves into visualizing distributions, cleaning and preprocessing messy data, and finally training models. Linear regression, logistic regression, and diabetes prediction with cross validation to check whether the results hold.
