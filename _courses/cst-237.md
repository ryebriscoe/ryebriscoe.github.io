Built a processor from the gate up, then programmed one directly. Six hardware labs in HDL starting with nothing but NAND, working through multiplexers and combinational logic to sequential chips. Then assembly on top of it, MIPS in the MARS simulator and x86 on the transfer side.

Two instruction sets and a processor built by hand. It is the clearest picture I have of what the hardware is actually doing underneath the code.
