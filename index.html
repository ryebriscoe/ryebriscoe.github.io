About me

I was twelve the first time I opened up a computer that mattered. My mom's MacBook had stopped charging, and I spent hours working through repair videos, stripping back the charger and holding specific wires together until the light came on. It ran for about five minutes before it sparked and I short-circuited the whole machine.

That should have put me off. Instead it did the opposite — I wanted to know exactly what I had gotten wrong. I started building PCs, and by thirteen I was pulling apart networks, running Wireshark, and learning what an IP address actually was because I wanted to understand the games I was playing. The curiosity never really switched off; it just got more formal.

I am finishing a B.S. in Computer Science at CSU Monterey Bay with a Software Engineering focus, graduating {{ site.school.graduation }}. Right now I am building B.A.D., a computer vision system that recognizes basketball plays from video, and working through a set of certifications in AI engineering and project management.

The program

In the CS Online program at CSUMB, students learn fundamental computer science concepts while applying them to real-world problems. The curriculum is heavily project-based, with collaboration, teamwork, and communication built into the coursework. Students complete a common core that establishes a foundation in programming, systems, and theory, then move into concentration coursework — in my case Software Engineering — before a culminating capstone project that integrates the whole degree.

Goals

My goal is to move into software engineering with a focus on applied machine learning — systems where a model is one component inside real infrastructure, not the whole product. B.A.D. is the clearest example of what I want to build: a computer vision pipeline feeding a vision-language model, evaluated honestly against a held-out answer key.

Alongside the degree I am working through the IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate, Generative AI with Large Language Models, and the Google Project Management Career Certificate, so that I graduate with production experience in the tools and not only coursework in the theory.
